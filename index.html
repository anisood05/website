PROFILE
DIGITAL LEADER

Anirudh is an ARB-registered architect operating with one foot in practice and one in digital delivery, translating what studios actually need into BIM, data and AI systems they can run at scale.

Since joining Aukett Swanke, he has progressed from BIM Specialist to Head of Digital in under three years, building the practice's internal tools, capability framework and AI governance from the ground up rather than importing them off the shelf.

He believes the next decade's real gains for the built environment will come not from software alone, but from architects and digital specialists working the problem together, on live projects, inside real practice constraints.
